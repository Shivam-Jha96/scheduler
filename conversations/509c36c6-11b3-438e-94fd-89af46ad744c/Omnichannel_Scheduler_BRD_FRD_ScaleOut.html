Business Requirements Document (BRD)

Formal business requirements, strategic vision, persona needs, SMART business objectives, unit economics, and operational risk mitigation policies.

1.0 Business Problem Statement

  • • Absence of Native Scheduling: Unlike Telegram or Slack, WhatsApp and Viber provide zero native 1-to-1 personal message scheduling.
  • • Fragility of Mobile Automation: Device-bound tools (Siri shortcuts, Android accessibility) fail when the device is locked, dead, or lacks network.
  • • Sender Identity Dilemma: Corporate WhatsApp Business APIs send from strange corporate numbers, confusing personal friends and triggering Meta's 24h window lockout.
  • • Account Suspension Risk: Unofficial spammers send bursts with no typing emulation, causing permanent SIM bans.

2.0 Strategic Value Proposition

Freelancers & Prosumers

Draft invoices and updates at midnight; deliver at 9:00 AM client local time from personal number.

Small Business Operators

Automate appointment reminders and weekly group cohort announcements without costly CRM licenses.

Remote Team Leads

Schedule daily standup prompts to distributed group chats Mon–Fri with automated holiday skips.

Everyday Individuals

Never miss birthdays, anniversaries, medication alerts, or recurring household reminders.

4.0 Core Business Rules

BR-01 Identity

Sender Authenticity

Outbound messages must originate from Alice's registered account session so Bob receives them directly in his existing 1:1 chat thread.

BR-02 Integrity

Zero-Double-Send Guarantee

Under no circumstance may a message be delivered more than once. Concurrent worker acquisitions must enforce transactional locks and Redis idempotency tokens.

BR-03 Security

Data Privacy & Encryption

Session credentials and message contents must be encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM. Personal data is scrubbed upon user account deletion.

BR-04 Policy

Human Emulation & Anti-Ban

Outbound socket transmissions must emit human typing presences (1.2–3.5s) and space consecutive dispatches by 3–6 seconds to prevent ban heuristics.

BR-05 Temporal

Wall-Clock DST Invariance

Scheduled times are anchored to the user's local IANA timezone. Seasonal daylight saving time shifts must not alter the user's perceived delivery hour.

FR-CLI-02 FR-CLI Interactive Queue Inspection (/list) Returns formatted message summary of all pending jobs with unique short IDs, recipients, and localized delivery times. P0
FR-CLI-03 FR-CLI Cancellation & In-Flight Eviction (/cancel) Marks database status as CANCELLED and actively removes buffered jobs from BullMQ delay queue using jobId eviction. P0
FR-SCHED-01 FR-SCHED Two-Tier Look-Ahead Feeder Engine Cron queries PostgreSQL every 60 seconds with FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED for jobs due in next 5 minutes and pushes to BullMQ with millisecond delay. P0
FR-SCHED-02 FR-SCHED Idempotency & Zero-Double-Send Guarantee Before socket dispatch, worker acquires a 10-minute Redis lock SET lock:msg:{id} NX EX 600. If lock fails, job is discarded immediately. P0
FR-REC-01 FR-REC Single-Active-Row Recurrence Architecture Recurrent schedules maintain exactly ONE active database row. When trigger occurs, system dispatches and immediately advances timestamp to next iteration. P0
FR-REC-02 FR-REC Wall-Clock DST Disambiguation Using Luxon timezone calculations, scheduled messages anchor to wall-clock time. Gaps during spring-forward advance to valid local time; fall-back folds use earlier occurrence. P0
FR-REL-01 FR-REL Exponential Backoff & Jitter Retries Transient socket errors retry up to 5 times with exponential backoff (2s, 4s, 8s, 16s, 32s) plus random 500ms jitter. P0
FR-REL-02 FR-REL Dead Letter Queue & Out-of-Band Notification Exhausted jobs move to DLQ with state FAILED_PERMANENTLY and send an alert notification back to Alice. P0
FR-META-01 FR-META Human Typing Emulation Worker sends composing presence for calculated duration (chars × 50ms, clamped 1.5s–4.0s) before transmitting message stanza. P0
FR-META-02 FR-META Inter-Message Jitter Spacing Outbound messages from the same sender are serialized with minimum 3.0-second delay to defeat algorithmic spam heuristics. P0
