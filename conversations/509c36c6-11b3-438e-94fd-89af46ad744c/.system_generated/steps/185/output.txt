The search for ""Viber" schedule message native personal chat" returned the following summary:
As of right now, **Viber does not have a native, built-in feature to schedule messages for personal chats** (one-on-one conversations). 

While Viber *does* support scheduled messages for **Viber Communities and Channels** (allowing admins to plan posts ahead of time)[1], that functionality is not available for regular, everyday personal messaging[2]. 

If you want to schedule or delay a message to someone in a personal Viber chat, you have to rely on workarounds depending on your device:

### Workarounds for Android
* **Third-Party Automation Apps:** Android users often use automation and task-scheduling apps (like *TikTask*[3] or similar notification/auto-send tools) to trigger automated text strings or reminders routed through Viber. 

### Workarounds for iPhone (iOS)
* **No Direct Integration:** iOS doesn't allow third-party apps to directly hook into and auto-send messages inside competing chat apps due to system sandboxing. 
* **Alternative Channels:** If scheduling is a strict requirement for a specific contact, you may need to switch to a platform that natively supports it for personal chats (such as Apple's *iMessage* via the "Send Later" feature[4], or Telegram).
