The search for ""Signal" app schedule send message native feature" returned the following summary:
Signal features a **native message scheduling** option, though its availability depends on your operating system[1]. 

### Platform Availability
* **Android:** Fully supported natively[1].
* **iOS / Desktop:** The native scheduling feature is primarily built around Android’s local alarm/background system[2]. 

---

### How to Use Native Message Scheduling (Android)

#### 1. To Schedule a Text Message:
1. Open a chat and type your message[3].
2. **Long-press (tap and hold) the Send icon**[3]. A popup will appear showing time presets as well as a **"Pick Date & Time"** option[1].
3. Select your desired delivery time and tap **Schedule send**[3].

#### 2. To Schedule Media (Images or Videos):
1. Tap the **`+`** icon in the chat and select **Gallery**[3].
2. Choose the photo or video you want to send[3].
3. **Long-press (tap and hold) the Send icon**, choose your time, and tap **Schedule send**[3].

#### 3. How to Manage, Cancel, or Reschedule:
* If you have pending scheduled messages, a notification banner or counter will appear right above your text input field. 
* Tap **See all** to view the list of queued messages[3].
* From there, you can delete/cancel them or tap the calendar icon next to a message to **Reschedule** it for a different time[3].

---

### Important Technical Details to Keep in Mind:
* **Local Queuing:** Because Signal is heavily focused on end-to-end encryption and privacy, scheduled messages are **stored locally on your device**, not on Signal's servers[4][5]. 
* **Internet Requirement:** Your phone **must be turned on and connected to the internet** at the exact time the message is scheduled to go out[3]. If your phone is offline or dead at that moment, the message will automatically send the next time your device reconnects[3].
* **System Permissions:** If the feature isn't working or failing to send, check your device settings to ensure Signal is allowed to set alarms and reminders (e.g., *Android Settings > Apps > Signal > Allow setting alarms and reminders*)[3], and that your phone is set to automatic date, time, and time zone[3].
