The search for ""WeChat" schedule message native personal chat" returned the following summary:
As of right now, **WeChat does not feature a native, built-in option to schedule messages for personal chats** (1-on-1 chats between individual users or standard group chats)[1]. 

However, depending on what you are trying to achieve, there are a few built-in alternatives or workaround paths:

### 1. The Native Alternative: WeChat "Chat Reminders"
If your goal is to remember to send a message yourself at a specific time rather than having the app send it automatically, WeChat has a built-in **Chat Reminders** feature:
1. Open any chat and **long-press** the specific message or content you want to reference[2].
2. Tap **Reminders** from the pop-up menu[2].
3. Choose a time frame (e.g., in 2 hours, tomorrow, or up to a month out)[2].
4. When the time arrives, WeChat will send you a Service Notification alert. Tapping it will take you directly back to that exact message context so you can type and send it manually[2].

### 2. Official Alternative for Business: WeChat Work (WeCom)
If you are communicating for business or professional purposes, Tencent’s companion app, **WeCom (WeChat Work)**, *does* feature a native scheduled messaging option[1]. You can link certain external WeChat contacts or chat within work groups, compose a message, and natively select a specific date and time for it to send automatically[1].

### 3. Workarounds for Regular Personal WeChat Chats
Because regular personal WeChat lacks a native scheduler, users looking to auto-send messages usually rely on external workarounds, though they come with security or privacy trade-offs[1]:
* **Third-Party Automation/Assistants:** Some users utilize third-party automation tools, auxiliary software, or "WeChat robots" designed to handle timed messaging[3][4]. *Note: Use caution with these, as unauthorized third-party modifications or extensions can violate WeChat’s Terms of Service and risk account suspension.*
* **Web Automation / Scripts:** Tech-savvy users sometimes script automated triggers using WeChat Web or developer tools via a browser, though WeChat frequently updates its security to block automated web sessions[3].
