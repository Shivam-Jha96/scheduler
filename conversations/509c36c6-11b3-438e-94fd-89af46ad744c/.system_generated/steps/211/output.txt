## 1. Executive Summary & Project Vision

### 1.1 Executive Summary
WhatsApp is the world's leading messaging application with over 2.7 billion active users. However, WhatsApp provides **no native capability for end users to schedule personal or group messages** for future delivery within the mobile client. Users are forced to rely on clumsy third-party accessibility hacks, external calendar alarms, or manual reminders.

The **WhatsApp Message Scheduler Bot** solves this friction by providing a seamless, in-chat scheduling engine. Operating directly within WhatsApp messaging threads, the bot enables any user (**Alice**) to schedule one-off or recurring messages to any personal contact or group chat (**Bob**).

### 1.2 Vision Statement
To deliver an intuitive, reliable, and privacy-compliant in-WhatsApp message scheduler that allows individuals and professionals to automate their communication without leaving WhatsApp, maintaining 100% peer-to-peer authenticity and zero-double-send reliability.

## 2. Business Problem Statement & Market Opportunity

### 2.1 The Problem
1. **Absence of Native Scheduling:** Unlike Telegram, Slack, or email clients, WhatsApp lacks native scheduled send capabilities for personal and small business accounts.
2. **Friction of External Tools:** Existing workarounds (e.g., Siri Shortcuts, Android Accessibility automation, external CRM platforms) require complex setup, compromise device battery, and fail when the phone is locked or powered off.
3. **The Sender Identity Dilemma:** Official WhatsApp Business Platform APIs originate messages from corporate Business Phone Numbers (WABA), confusing personal contacts and triggering Meta's strict 24-hour service window block (`Error 131047`).
4. **Reliability & Ban Hazards:** Unofficial tools often send messages instantly without human pacing, triggering algorithmic SIM bans and resulting in lost client communication.

### 2.2 Market Opportunity & Value Proposition
- **Prosumers & Freelancers:** Schedule client follow-ups, invoices, and timezone-adjusted check-ins.
- **Small Business Owners & Service Providers:** Automate appointment reminders, status updates, and operational notices without paying exorbitant enterprise CRM licensing.
- **Personal Convenience:** Never forget birthdays, anniversaries, medication reminders, or group chat standups.

   - Two-tier hybrid scheduler (PostgreSQL 16 master + BullMQ 5-minute sliding buffer).

## 8. Business Risks & Mitigation Strategy

1. **Meta Terms of Service Enforcement on Personal Accounts:**
   - *Risk:* Meta algorithmically flags rapid automated socket activity.
   - *Mitigation:* Multi-modal human typing simulation, burst-rate limiters, single-phone pairing codes, and instant failover to Model A (Assistive CTA Redirector).
2. **User Timezone Misconfiguration:**
   - *Risk:* User sets reminder for 9:00 AM but intends local time while bot assumes UTC.
   - *Mitigation:* The onboarding flow and scheduling wizard explicitly display and confirm the active IANA timezone (`America/New_York`, `Asia/Kolkata`, etc.) on every step.
3. **Database Node Failure During Scheduled Spikes:**
   - *Risk:* Power loss or node crash at 8:59 AM causes scheduled 9:00 AM jobs to be lost.
   - *Mitigation:* PostgreSQL Write-Ahead Logging (WAL) + synchronous commit ensures durability. Upon reboot, the Two-Tier Feeder sweeps and fast-forwards overdue jobs within 60 seconds.
