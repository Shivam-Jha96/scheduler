Instagram features a **native direct message (DM) scheduling** option that allows you to queue up text messages to send at a later date and time directly inside the mobile app[1][2][3]. 

### How to Natively Schedule an Instagram DM:
1. Open the **Instagram app** on your iOS or Android device[4].
2. Go to your **Direct Messages (DMs)** inbox and open the conversation where you want to send the message[5].
3. Type out your message in the text box as you normally would[6]. 
4. Instead of tapping normally, **long-press (press and hold) the Send button**[6].
5. A scheduling menu/pop-up will appear[6]. Use the options to pick your desired **date and time**, then confirm[7].

### Key Details & Limitations:
* **Timeframe:** You can typically schedule messages up to **29 days in advance**[8][6].
* **Content Type:** The native feature primarily supports **text-only** messages[2][3]. 
* **Managing Scheduled Messages:** Once scheduled, a notification banner (e.g., *"1 scheduled message"*) will appear at the bottom of the chat[7][6]. You can tap it to view upcoming scheduled messages, or long-press an individual scheduled bubble to **Send now, Copy text, or Delete** it[7][4].
