LINE features a native **Message Scheduler** feature (released via LINE Labs)[1]. 

### How to Turn On the Message Scheduler Feature
To use scheduled messaging, you first need to enable it in LINE Labs on your mobile device (requires LINE version 26.17.0 or later)[2]:
1. Open the **LINE app** on your main device.
2. Go to the **Home** tab and tap **Settings** (the gear icon) in the top-right corner[1][2].
3. Tap **LINE Labs**[1].
4. Toggle on **Message scheduler** (or *Scheduled Send*)[1][2].

---

### How to Schedule a Message
1. Open any 1-on-1 chat, multi-person chat, group chat, or your **Keep Memo**[1].
2. Type out your text message[1].
3. Select the option to **Schedule message**[2].
4. Choose your preferred **date and time** for the message to be sent, then confirm[2].

---

### Key Rules, Limits, and Conditions
* **Time Range:** You can schedule messages anywhere from **5 minutes to 1 year** in advance (in 1-minute increments)[1].
* **Supported Content:** Text messages (up to 10,000 characters, including URL links, mentions, and emojis)[1][2]. 
* **Account Limits:** You can have up to **500 active scheduled messages** per account at a time[1].
* **Device Restriction:** Setting up scheduled messages must be done via the **mobile app** (it is not natively supported on the desktop version yet, though recipients will receive them normally on desktop)[2].
* **Managing/Editing:** You can view, edit, or delete your pending scheduled messages before they are automatically dispatched[2]. 
* **Important Caveat:** If you turn off the feature inside LINE Labs while messages are still pending, those scheduled messages will automatically be deleted[1]. Additionally, this feature is rolling out as an experimental LINE Labs / trial feature headed toward paid/subscription tier integration (such as LYP Premium)[3][2].
